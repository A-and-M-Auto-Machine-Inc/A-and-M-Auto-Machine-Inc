About A & M Auto Machine Inc

Since 1999, A & M Auto Machine has been proudly serving the Meriden, CT area with top-quality engine rebuilding services. Our team is skilled, dedicated, and passionate about what we do.

The engine is the most important part of your vehicle — and we rebuild it the right way. With nearly 20 years of service and hundreds of successful rebuilds under our belts, we've earned a reputation for excellence.

We work on all types of engines: foreign, domestic, antique, agricultural, industrial, and marine. No job is too big or too small.
